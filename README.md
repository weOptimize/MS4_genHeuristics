🔍 What’s in this work for your organization? 🔍 
📈 Optimized Decision-Making: combine the search for profitability without missing important non-quantifiable information.
🎯 Strategic Alignment: save time in strategic analysis by excluding misaligned portfolios from the shortlist.
🌍 Enhanced Realism: we tested our methodology with real-world data from the LIFE program from the European Commission.
📚 It has been published by Springer in the proceedings of the Second Decision Science Alliance International Summer Conference, DSA ISC 2024, held in Valencia, Spain, in June 2024. The book was co-edited by Angel A. Juan, Javier Faulin, and David López.
🙏 I thank them and Laura Calvet, as co-author, for this opportunity to contribute to the field. We look forward to applying it in organizations that may benefit from this approach and to performing further research because we see high potential in this type of generative heuristic approaches.
